📱 Your direct channel · Tours

Online ordering under your name, for your restaurant in Tours

A real ordering site of your own, not a listing lost in a giant's directory. Captain.Food wants to give the restaurants of Tours a direct channel, without a commission gnawing at your margin.

⚓ Nothing is built yet. We're just starting out in Tours with the first restaurateurs. This page describes what we want to build, not a service already online. You can already try the mockups and tell us what's missing.

A ~30% commission on a ~3% net margin: up to 10× what you keep on the order.

Your site, your name, your rules

The idea: your own ordering storefront, like yourresto.captain.food (or your own domain yourresto.fr as an option). Orders would come in direct, under your name, in three ways — all commission-free on your dishes:

  • By delivery (a channel to come), paid decently.
  • To take away, via click and collect: the customer orders ahead and picks up on site.
  • At the table, by QR code: the customer scans and orders from their seat; payment at the till stays in your usual takings.

0% commission: the math it changes

On a classic platform, the commission sits around ~25 to 30% (public sources, 2024), while the average net margin of a restaurant is about ~3% (Observatoire Fiducial, UMIH/GHR/SNARR 2025). An illustrative example: on a €20 order, a platform can take ~€6; through your Captain.Food online ordering, it would be €0 on the dish — you keep your €20.

The service would be funded by a pay-what-you-want customer contribution (€0 is fine, no judgment), never by you, never a surcharge imposed on the customer. How we fund ourselves, in plain terms →

Your customers become your customers again

The most underrated point: on the big platforms, you don't even know who's ordering from you. Here, every order placed directly belongs to you — you get access to your customers' contact details (with their consent) to build loyalty yourself. Your customer base becomes an asset of yours again, not a third party's.

And nothing to install in a rush: we want to support the setup at your own pace (syncing your menu, putting your page online). No machine to learn in a panic.

The idea is that you get your own ordering site, like yourresto.captain.food (or your own domain yourresto.fr as an option), not a listing drowned in someone else's directory. Orders come in direct, under your name, with no commission on your dishes.
