A commission-free restaurant in Tours — that's the course Captain.Food is set on

Keeping 100% of the price of your dishes isn't a marketing promise: it's the very heart of the model we want to build for the independent restaurants of Tours.

⚓ Nothing is built yet. Captain.Food is just starting out in Tours. This page describes what we want to build together, not a service that's already available.

A ~30% commission on a ~3% net margin: up to 10× what you keep on the order.

Why “commission-free” changes everything

The average net margin of a restaurant sits around ~3% (Observatoire Fiducial, UMIH/GHR/SNARR 2025). When a delivery platform takes ~25 to 30% commission (public sources, 2024), it can capture ten times your margin on that order. The result: either you inflate your online prices (and customers order less), or you swallow it (and your margin melts away). Passing the commission on doesn't cancel it — it makes your revenue pay for it.

A commission-free channel breaks that circle. An illustrative example: on a €30 order, a ~30% platform can take ~€9; with Captain.Food, it would be €0 on the dish — you keep your €30. See the funding in detail, example included →

So who pays? (real transparency)

The service would be funded by a pay-what-you-want customer contribution, added to their order at a free price: everyone gives what they want to keep a local alternative alive. A customer doesn't contribute? That's fine, no judgment: no contribution on that order, and still 0 commission for you. Card payment fees are the standard banking fees of any online payment — never a percentage on your dishes, never a surcharge added for the customer.

So you don't have to take our word for it, we open our accounts to the public on Open Collective: every euro received and spent meant to be visible. The page fills in as we move forward.

What it brings you, concretely

  • 0% commission on your dishes, always.
  • Your customer base belongs to you — recover your customers from the first direct order.
  • Your prices, no double menu: the same as dine-in.
  • One channel for online ordering, click and collect and, down the line, ethical delivery.

The legal framework follows the same logic: a structure carried by the Caring Hope Foundation association (a French loi 1901 non-profit), with a cooperative course (SCIC as the goal) and ESUS accreditation as the objective. Frequently asked questions

Without a commission, how does Captain.Food fund itself?

Through a pay-what-you-want customer contribution, added to their order in full transparency (€0 is fine, no judgment). Never a percentage on your margin, never a surcharge imposed on the customer. Card fees are the standard banking fees of any online payment. The accounts are open to the public on Open Collective.

Is the 0% commission really guaranteed over time?

It's the golden rule and the heart of the model: 0% on your dishes, no matter what. Since we take no commission, we don't have that lever to squeeze you later. The structure is aiming for cooperative governance (SCIC as the goal) and ESUS accreditation (an objective) to give that commitment a legal frame.
